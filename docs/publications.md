### Empirical evidence documented in the FIE 2025 study

The FIE 2025 contribution is especially relevant to the JULIUS open-source narrative because it reports an actual educational experience rather than only describing the tool.

The published abstract identifies the work as a **full research-to-practice paper** and reports a **mixed-methods methodology**, integrating quantitative pre- and post-tests with qualitative thematic analysis.

Reported outcomes include significant improvements in conceptual understanding, logical reasoning, motivation and collaboration, together with improvements in emotional well-being, metacognition and teamwork.

The paper therefore provides empirical support for positioning JULIUS as an educational intervention that has been tested in an authentic programming-learning context.

#### Numerical-results policy for this repository

The repository should not reproduce specific percentages, means, p-values, sample sizes or effect sizes unless they are taken directly from the full IEEE paper or from the original research dataset. The currently accessible bibliographic abstract confirms the design and direction of the results but does not provide the complete numerical tables.
