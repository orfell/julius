# Claude for Open Source — Project Rationale

## Project identity

**JULIUS + Traffic Light Method**

JULIUS is a research-backed educational AI assistant for programming education. It is paired with the Traffic Light Method, a structured methodology for analyzing inputs, processes and outputs before evaluating pseudocode or generating feedback.

The project should therefore be understood as an **open educational methodology plus AI-assisted tooling**, not merely as a standalone chatbot.

## Evidence of sustained educational work

The project grows from a documented sequence of educational innovation:

- **2024 — Unicomfacauca Edutech Evolutiva:** the project "Integración de IA generativa a través de chatbots en el aula Unicomfacauca" received third place among the selected educational innovation prototypes. The winning teams received a paid academic internship at Universidad de La Sabana.
- **2025 — EDUNINE:** JULIUS was presented as a virtual tutor used with a seven-step methodology for strengthening logical and algorithmic thinking.
- **2025 — FIE:** JULIUS was evaluated in a real educational experience using mixed methods, including quantitative pre/post assessment and qualitative thematic analysis.
- **2026 — EDUNINE:** the methodology was explicitly presented as the **Traffic Light Method**, supporting teacher-supervised assessment through analysis of inputs, processes and outputs, personalized reports, group indicators and error-pattern identification.

## Academic and community mission

This project is fundamentally **academic, scientific and educational**. JULIUS and the Traffic Light Method are not being developed as commercial software products. Their purpose is to contribute knowledge, methods, classroom evidence and reusable educational practices to the academic community.

The work has been presented through recognized international engineering-education venues and institutional innovation initiatives. The project seeks to continue that trajectory by creating additional AI-enabled classroom tools, documenting them openly and sharing the resulting methodology and evidence with educators and researchers.

Claude support would therefore be used to accelerate **non-commercial educational research and open academic development**: prototyping, documentation, reproducibility, code improvement, classroom examples, responsible AI workflows and teacher-supervised assessment.

The project is still evolving and should be understood as a scientific contribution in active development, already applied in authentic educational contexts with reported results.


## Why Claude is relevant

The purpose of seeking support through **Claude for Open Source** is to continue creating and documenting AI tools for the classroom using Claude as part of the development workflow.

Potential uses include:

- development and refactoring of open educational AI prototypes;
- documentation of the Traffic Light Method;
- creation of reproducible classroom examples;
- testing of teacher-supervised AI workflows;
- generation and review of code, prompts and instructional materials;
- exploration of Claude-based assistants for programming education;
- preparation of open research artifacts that educators can reproduce and improve.

## Human supervision

The project follows a human-in-the-loop principle:

**The AI proposes, organizes and supports; the teacher reviews and decides.**

JULIUS is not intended to function as an autonomous grading authority.

## Current Open Source position

JULIUS is an early-stage public repository. It does **not** currently claim the large adoption metrics listed in Anthropic's standard eligibility examples, such as 20 external contributors or 100 merged pull requests into third-party repositories.

Anthropic's current program also states that maintainers who do not exactly fit the published thresholds may still apply when they maintain something the ecosystem depends on. This application therefore presents JULIUS on the basis of its documented educational research trajectory, public methodology, institutional validation and goal of becoming a reusable open educational AI project.
