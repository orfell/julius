# Intellectual Property, Attribution and Academic Sharing

## Purpose

JULIUS and the Traffic Light Method are being developed as academic and scientific contributions for the educational community.

## Copyright status

The maintainer reports a Colombian copyright-related registration/protection for JULIUS as an innovative methodology.

A public web search performed while preparing this repository did not surface a verifiable registration number or public certificate. For that reason, this repository intentionally avoids publishing an unverified registration identifier.

When the official certificate, registration number or public record is available, it should be added to this document.

## Academic sharing

The project's goal is not to commercialize JULIUS as a software product. The aim is to:

- preserve authorship and attribution;
- share educational methodology and evidence;
- support reproducibility;
- encourage responsible academic reuse;
- enable collaboration among educators and researchers;
- continue developing AI tools for teaching and learning.

## Relationship between copyright and open source

Copyright establishes authorship and legal ownership. An open-source or open-education license can later define how others may reuse, adapt and redistribute specific materials.

A license should only be selected after confirming ownership and redistribution rights for all code, media, institutional content and third-party assets contained in the repository.
