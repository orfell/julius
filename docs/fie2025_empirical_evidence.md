# JULIUS — Empirical Evidence Summary

## FIE 2025

**Title:** Enhancing Algorithmic Thinking and Emotional Resilience in Programming Education Through AI Powered Virtual Tutoring

**Authors:** Julio R. Martinez, Mario Chong, Susana Maya, María Alejandra Caicedo, Ana Luna

**Venue:** 55th IEEE Annual Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE 2025)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/FIE63693.2025.11328240

## Why this paper is central to JULIUS

This publication presents JULIUS as an AI-powered virtual tutor used in a Fundamentals of Programming context. It is a full research-to-practice paper and reports an empirical educational experience.

## Methodology reported in the public bibliographic record

- Mixed-methods approach.
- Quantitative pre-test and post-test.
- Qualitative thematic analysis.
- Connectivism as a theoretical foundation.
- Challenge-Based Learning (CBL).
- Use of tools such as Excel and PSeInt.
- Structured group dynamics.

## Results reported in the public abstract

The publication reports significant improvements in:

- conceptual understanding;
- logical reasoning;
- motivation;
- collaboration.

It also reports improvements in:

- emotional well-being;
- metacognition;
- teamwork;
- student confidence and autonomy;
- support for adaptive and collaborative learning.

## Interpretation for the open-source project

These results allow JULIUS to be documented as a research-backed educational intervention with evidence from an authentic learning experience, not merely as a software prototype.

## Important evidence rule

Do not add exact percentages, sample size, means, test statistics, p-values or effect sizes to GitHub until they are verified against the full IEEE paper or the original research dataset. The public abstract currently available confirms the methodology and direction of the findings, but not all underlying numerical values.
