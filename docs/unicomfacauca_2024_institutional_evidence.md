# JULIUS — Institutional Experience and Recognition at Unicomfacauca

## Edutech Evolutiva 2024

In 2024, Unicomfacauca conducted the faculty training initiative **Edutech Evolutiva: creación de prototipos de innovaciones educativas con Inteligencia Artificial**, led by Ph.D. Andrés Chiappe Laverde.

The institutional news published by Unicomfacauca on 8 November 2024 reports that the training lasted two months and concluded with the recognition of the three best educational innovation prototypes.

The project:

**“Integración de IA generativa a través de chatbots en el aula Unicomfacauca”**

was awarded **third place**.

Team:
- Julio Ricardo Martínez Montezuma
- Susana Eugenia Maya Durán
- María Alejandra Caicedo Bucheli

The three winning teams received a **paid academic internship at Universidad de La Sabana**.

Institutional source:
https://www.unicomfacauca.edu.co/el-iii-congreso-de-ciencias-sociales-y-humanas-se-consolido-como-una-experiencia-innovadora-sostenible-y-a-la-vanguardia-tecnologica/

## Academic internship in Bogotá

A second institutional article, published on 29 November 2024, confirms that the nine winning professors participated in an academic visit to **Universidad de La Sabana** and **Universidad del Rosario**.

For the JULIUS-related team, Unicomfacauca again identifies the project as:

**“Integración de IA generativa a través de chatbots en el aula Unicomfacauca”**

and lists:
- Julio Ricardo Martínez Montezuma
- Susana Eugenia Maya Durán
- María Alejandra Caicedo Bucheli

At Universidad de La Sabana, the team interacted with leaders of the Doctorate in Educational Innovation and with living labs focused on design, prototyping, coworking and neuroscience applied to education.

Institutional source:
https://www.unicomfacauca.edu.co/profesores-de-unicomfacauca-fortalecen-competencias-en-innovacion-educativa-con-pasantia-en-bogota/

## Connection with JULIUS

This institutional recognition is relevant to the JULIUS project because it documents an internal innovation process at Unicomfacauca around the integration of generative AI and chatbots in teaching.

It complements the later peer-reviewed evidence from EDUNINE 2025 and FIE 2025, where JULIUS is presented and evaluated as an AI-powered educational tutor for programming education.

The evidence trail can therefore be described as:

**Institutional educational innovation (2024) → recognized prototype → academic internship at Universidad de La Sabana → international dissemination and empirical evaluation (2025) → teacher-supervised assessment evolution (2026).**

## Evidence caution

The institutional news confirms the award, team members, project title and internship. It should not be used to claim specific JULIUS performance metrics unless those metrics come from the corresponding research paper or validated institutional study.
